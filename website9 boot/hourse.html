About Our
Farm

At K. Valdez, we pride ourselves on offering a captivating experience for horse enthusiasts. Nestled in a picturesque rural setting, our farm provides top-notch boarding, expert lessons, and breathtaking trail rides. Our commitment to the well-being of our horses and riders shines through in every interaction and service we offer.

Services

Horse Boarding Services

At our horse farm, we offer premium boarding services to ensure your horse receives the highest level of care. Whether you're looking for short-term or long-term boarding, our facilities are designed with your horse's comfort and safety in mind

Training and Riding Lessons

Our farm is home to experienced trainers who specialize in a variety of disciplines, including western, dressage, and jumping. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced rider, we offer personalized training sessions to help you reach your goals

Horse Rehabilitation and Care

We understand that horses may need extra care from time to time, which is why we offer rehabilitation services for injured or recovering horses. Our facility is equipped with everything necessary for a smooth recovery, from therapeutic riding sessions to physical therapy exercises
